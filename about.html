ABOUT IMPORT OUTLET 90s

Welcome to Import Outlet 90s, your destination for Japanese vehicles from the 1990s and 2000s. We focus on bringing together a selection of interesting Japanese imports, ranging from performance-focused vehicles to well-equipped everyday road cars.

OUR BACKGROUND

Japanese vehicles have developed a strong reputation for their engineering, reliability and distinctive styling. From iconic performance models to practical family vehicles, Japanese manufacturers have produced a wide variety of cars that continue to attract enthusiasts and everyday motorists.

Import Outlet 90s was created around an appreciation for this period of Japanese automotive history. Our aim is to showcase a varied selection of vehicles from this era in one convenient place.
